About

In the field, not behind a screen.

I play LARP the way it is meant to be played: on my feet, in costume, with a story that has to survive weather, improvisation, and other people. The work is physical and social — voice, stance, kit, and the patience to let a scene breathe.

Most of my time goes into long-form campaigns: factions with real politics, characters with debts, and weekends that feel like a chapter rather than a skirmish. When I am not in kit I am usually mending leather, writing briefs, helping new players find their first good scene, or posting the after-action on LinkedIn.
